FAQs

Answers to common questions

Clear answers. Total confidence. Everything you need before you order.

Contact our team →
How long does production take?

5–7 business days for normal production. For a rush fee we can make it within 2–3 business days and ship.

Can my graphics be etched into the stock chain?

No, we don’t offer that on our stocks. All graphic work is a vinyl on top of the metal.

How long to make a fully custom premier chain?

After artwork is approved and the deposit is received (half of the invoice to start production), normally 3–4 weeks — not 12 weeks like other companies.

How much do fully custom premier chains cost?

There are lots of variations, so we email you a price guide. They range from $450 to $1,300.

How much for shipping?

Shipping varies by destination but is low and insured. We’re located in Ohio and generally ship UPS — UPS Ground may be only $16.99 with insurance. We can also ship internationally; contact us for prices.
